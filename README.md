# Gravepedia

Gravepedia is a community memorial directory for searching published cemetery records and submitting sourced memorial information for review. Submissions remain private until they are approved.

## What we offer

- Search published records by name, cemetery, or burial place.
- Submit a memorial with dates, inscription, notes, and a source for review.
- Pending submissions are stored in GitHub-backed data and stay out of public search until approved.

## How it works

1. **Search** — Enter a name, cemetery, or place to find matching published memorials.
2. **Contribute** — Submit sourced details for a memorial. Submissions are held for review before publication.

## Who it's for

- Families preserving memories and family trees.
- Genealogists and researchers seeking primary burial evidence.
- Local historians and community volunteers documenting cemeteries.
- Anyone who wants to honor and remember people who have passed.

## Our values

- **Preserve:** Protect fragile information before it fades.
- **Respect:** Treat memorials with sensitivity and honor living relatives' privacy.
- **Community:** Collaboration and verification keep records accurate and meaningful.
- **Accessibility:** Make memorial information easy to find and use from any device.

## Get involved
